Hosted flow driving

Every built-in document family ORYH's agent is permitted to advance is subscribed here by default, with its driver skill and work queue derived from this company's own skills and lifecycles. This page is where you switch one off — the company having its own agent for quotations, say. Nothing is switched back on by a deploy.

Subscribed is not the same as running. A row only polls once three things hold: it is switched on here, this company has a hosted credential for the runner to act as, and the customer has published the workflow definition for that object type. The map is what starts the work, and writing it is the customer's — a subscription without one spends nothing and does nothing. The state column says which of the three is missing.
