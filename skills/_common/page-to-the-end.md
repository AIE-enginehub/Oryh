`size=200` is one page: read `meta.total` and page until you hold every
record — a trail on a later page is not an absent trail, and routing a
returned document as a fresh submission is how it gets approved. The detail
reads depend on nothing but the queue you already hold, so they go out as a
single batch; a queue of thirty is one wait, not sixty. Ask per document
(`&entity_id=`) only when the trail runs to many pages.
