Every entry names a project. A week totals the scheduled hours — 40 unless the person is part-time — and overtime is recorded as its own entry with a note saying why.

<!-- zh -->
每条工时必须关联到具体项目。一周合计为排班时数——非兼职为 40 小时——加班单独记一行并注明原因。
