Every receipt is attached and made out to the company's full legal name. A receipt older than 90 days is not reimbursable. A meal above 200 per person names who attended.

<!-- zh -->
每张票据都要上传，抬头必须是公司全称。开票超过 90 天的票据不予报销。人均超过 200 的餐费注明参加人员。
