Prices say whether tax is included. Validity does not exceed 30 days. A discount deeper than 10% carries a stated reason. A giveaway is a gift line, never a 100% discount.

<!-- zh -->
价格注明是否含税。有效期不超过 30 天。折扣超过 10% 的写明原因。赠品记为赠品行，不记为 100% 折扣。
