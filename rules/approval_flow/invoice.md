Sales invoices: finance reviews before issue. Supplier bills: the buyer confirms the three-way match, then finance approves; a variance above 1% of the order needs the purchasing manager before finance. Payslips: the HR manager approves the batch. The last approval is final.

<!-- zh -->
销项发票：开具前由财务审核。供应商账单：采购员确认三单匹配后由财务审批；与订单差异超过 1% 的，先经采购经理再到财务。工资条：由人事经理批整批。最后一个通过即为最终。
