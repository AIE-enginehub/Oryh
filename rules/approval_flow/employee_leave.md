The direct manager approves. More than five consecutive working days, or any request over the available balance, is countersigned by HR after the manager. The last approval is final: set the request to approved.

直属经理审批。连续超过五个工作日，或超出可用余额的请假，经理通过后由人事会签。最后一个通过即为最终：请假单置为 approved。
