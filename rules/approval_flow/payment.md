The finance manager approves every outbound payment. Above 50,000 the general manager countersigns after the finance manager. A payment whose counterparty account differs from the vendor's own record goes back to the filer before any approval. The last approval is final.

财务经理审批每一笔对外付款。金额超过 50000 的，财务经理通过后由总经理会签。收款账户与供应商档案不一致的付款，先退回填写人，再进入审批。最后一个通过即为最终。
