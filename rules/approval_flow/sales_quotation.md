title: Sales quotation approval flow
visibility: internal

A quotation at list price, or with every discount within 10%, needs no approval: calibrate and set it to approved. A discount deeper than 10% on any line needs the sales manager; deeper than 20%, the general manager countersigns after the sales manager. The last approval is final; sending is the salesperson's own move.
